# App Social Campaign

Turn verified product context into a coherent campaign and finished publishing assets. Adapt the creative direction to the app. The InTouch example demonstrates the process; its colors, copy, audiences, pricing, and feature claims are not defaults for other products.

## Match the requested scope

- **Explore or plan:** develop positioning, campaign structure, and concrete visual samples when useful. Do not silently turn a discussion into full production.
- **Create a campaign:** choose a coherent direction and produce the requested deliverables. For a new direction, recommend a small representative sample first; this is a collaboration default, not an extra approval requirement. If the user already authorized full production or supplied an approved direction, continue through delivery.
- **Continue or revise:** inspect the existing board, source, and approved exports. Reuse accepted decisions; change only the requested scope. A copy fix must not become an unrequested redesign.
- Publishing, scheduling, account changes, creator outreach, and paid spend are separate actions; campaign production alone does not authorize them.

## Learn the app before writing the campaign

Resolve the intended project and applicable instructions. Read referenced tasks through the host's task-reading tool before relying on them. Inspect current product docs, listing copy, release evidence, screenshots, design tokens, fonts, icon, and any existing social posts. Read source only where needed to resolve a claim; avoid a broad code audit.

Create or reuse a **project-local app profile**: audience hypothesis, problem, promise, core loop, supported platforms, voice, visual tokens, real assets, supported claims with evidence, release state, and CTA destination. Keep app-specific knowledge with that app so later campaigns inherit it without contaminating another product. Use [references/brief.md](references/brief.md) for the compact profile and strategy outline.

Ask only for missing choices that materially change the work: language, account identity/type, campaign goal, constraints, or launch timing. Reuse answers already supplied. Proceed with independent work while waiting. An unknown handle or release date need not block asset creation; use explicit publication conditions and relative dates.

Distinguish implemented, captured, submitted, and publicly available. Verify drift-prone public claims before publication. Do not invent features, metrics, reviews, founder stories, pricing, or availability. A screenshot of a selection is not evidence of a completed action.

## Build one campaign idea

Lead with a recognisable moment and a concrete benefit. Give each post one job, one takeaway, and a fitting next action. Translate features into useful consequences while keeping app control names accurate.

Mix recognition, standalone usefulness, product proof, trust/objections, and a small first action. Choose the mix for this product. Four weeks and three posts per week is a starting option, not a quota or an algorithm claim. Do not force every campaign into seven carousels and four Reels.

Design the campaign as a sequence that teaches a new visitor what the app is, why it matters, and how to start. Include per-post hooks, slide/scene copy, captions, supporting Stories, publication conditions, and the result to observe. Use a realistic cadence and small-account learning plan; do not promise reach or confuse correlation with acquisition attribution.

## Establish and preserve the visual direction

Use the app's visual identity or its approved social identity. Inspect real reference images. Set typography, palette, composition, screenshot treatment, brand mark, and spacing before multiplying layouts. A useful sample covers an emotional hook, product demonstration, and action/trust post; choose fewer when enough.

Keep screenshots authentic. Place editorial copy outside the app UI. Do not fabricate taps, saves, notifications, messages, selected states, or testimonials. Label conceptual artwork clearly. If a capture is stale or missing, obtain a suitable authorized capture or choose a truthful treatment that does not require it.

After the direction is accepted, record the decision and hash the approved exports. Preserve those files during expansion unless the user requests changes. Use one source of truth for all visible copy, captions, alt text, the review board, and production briefs. Include large numbers, list items, CTAs, and qualifying footer text in accessible text equivalents.

For a visual explanation of the approach, read [references/intouch-example.md](references/intouch-example.md) and inspect its bundled overview. This is optional inspiration, not a dependency on the old repository or conversation.

## Produce the requested campaign

Read [references/production.md](references/production.md) when making assets. It covers reusable helpers, local runtime discovery, screenshots, motion, the review board, verification, and packaging.

Typical complete delivery: finished feed artwork, actual MP4s for promised Reels, covers, Story images, captions, alt text/text equivalents, publishing order, profile kit where relevant, an observation log, editable source, a local review board, and one ZIP. Scale this to the request; do not add formats just to fill a checklist.

Use the appropriate available production tool. Local HTML/CSS suits precise editorial typography and authentic screenshot composition; use image generation for original illustrations/photos when helpful, following its tool instructions. Respect an explicitly requested editor. Do not silently substitute a local file for an explicitly requested Canva or other native design.

When footage is unavailable, a motion explainer using real screenshots can be a complete Reel if the treatment fits the requested campaign. Make that choice visible. Storyboards alone are not finished videos. Plan for sound-off comprehension; use original or appropriately licensed sound, and do not claim a listening review from technical audio checks.

## Verify and hand off

Inspect the finished images at full size and approximate feed size, plus the campaign contact sheet. Check each Reel's scene sequence, transitions, text timing, and actual playback. Automated dimensions or HTML bounds do not prove visual quality.

Check expected assets, image dimensions/color/alpha, video decoding and metadata, caption consistency, accessible copy, approved-file hashes, links, mobile layout, copy controls, video play/close behavior, and archive integrity as relevant. Technical helpers report technical evidence only. Fix failures within the authorized scope and rerun affected checks; do not repeat unaffected work.

Deliver the board and ZIP with a short count of finished outputs, material limitations, and the next publishing condition. Keep planned, produced, approved, scheduled, and published states distinct. Do not label a missing asset finished or an unpublished campaign launched.
