# Worked example: InTouch

This is a creative and production example approved by the user in September 2026. It is not evidence that this marketing campaign has performed well or that the product's old release status remains current.

Inspect [intouch-overview.png](intouch-overview.png) for the visual sequence.

## Why the approach worked for the request

- **Familiar moment:** “I should text them” is understandable before someone knows the app.
- **Small promise:** “A little help remembering” fits a manual relationship reminder app without promising to fix friendships.
- **Actual proof:** authentic People, History, Add a person, Log interactions, and cropped Settings captures support specific capabilities.
- **Value without installing:** message ideas and a shareable “Just thought of you” card give people something useful.
- **Trust:** clear limits on notifications, local storage, and manual logging answer likely objections.
- **Coherent identity:** Caprasimo, Figtree, cream, terracotta, sage, peach, and a small recurring mark came from this app. They should not migrate to an unrelated app by default.

## Sequence

| Role | Example hook |
|---|---|
| Recognition | I should text them. |
| Core product | A little help keeping in touch. |
| Feature proof | When did we last talk? |
| Useful message idea | No opening paragraph required. |
| Trust | A nudge. Then room to breathe. |
| Small action | Start with three people. |

Production proceeded from three finished sample carousels to the approved full direction. The final four-week pack contained seven four-slide carousels, four 20.9-second motion Reels, one single image, 24 Stories, covers, profile artwork, captions, accessible text, a local board, and a ZIP. These counts are an example, not a required recipe.

## Carry these lessons forward

- Share a reviewable sample before expensive expansion when the creative direction is undecided; continue immediately when the user has already approved full production.
- Preserve approved images by hash. Small corrections should not silently alter the visual language.
- Static screenshots can support a truthful motion explainer. They cannot become proof of recorded interactions through animation.
- Use visible copy outside tiny phone UI to explain the benefit. Crop actual UI details without redrawing product states.
- Keep accessible copy complete: a large standalone “2” or “3,” bullet items, and qualifying footers are content too.
- Maintain one copy source, export clean RGB PNGs, verify video decoding and playback, test the local board, and package all promised deliverables.

The bundled overview and this walkthrough are the public example. The original app's full campaign is maintained separately; the skill's workflow and helpers do not depend on that repository. For your own app, keep the media, copy, editable source, and review board together in its campaign directory, and rebuild delivery archives from those sources.
