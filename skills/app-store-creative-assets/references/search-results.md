# Search-results creative

Read when producing a dedicated search-results asset. A screenshot gallery that may also appear in search is a different deliverable.

## Show one reason to care

Lead with a supported benefit and a recognizable product experience. Reuse the approved opening screenshot's message when it makes search and the product page coherent. This is a useful starting point, not a requirement to repeat copy verbatim. An abstract header scene may not explain the app quickly enough in search; assess it at the intended small scale.

Choose one dominant visual. A large real app screen with a short headline and recognizable icon/name often works; product content or gameplay can be better for another app. Do not make one-phone layouts, two-line headlines, underlines or left/right placement universal rules.

When brand identity is part of the composition, use the actual current icon and canonical app-name case. Size them for recognition in the thumbnail, not just the full-resolution canvas. Decorative forms should come from this app's identity and support its purpose; avoid importing motifs from another app simply because their layout worked.

## Spend pixels on the benefit

Use a genuine capture whose visible state supports the headline. Keep the important UI large enough to recognize. A complete phone may be appropriate; deliberate bottom bleed can give the key content more room when the chosen treatment allows it. Preserve uniform scaling and distinguish the allowed hardware crop from accidentally clipping a needed title, dose status, countdown or card.

Fit the longest required language before multiplying exports. Reduce ornament or adjust spacing, wrapping and type size before compromising approved wording. Do not promise that every secondary UI label will be readable at thumbnail size: choose which product evidence must survive and inspect that explicitly.

A 360 px wide preview is a useful review default for compact search artwork. Inspect it at actual size alongside a larger view; it is not an Apple-prescribed display width. If the user specifies another display size, test that too. When the essential content is too small, simplify the composition or use a better real capture instead of fabricating enlarged interface fragments.

Keep important text and imagery comfortably away from edges and rounded corners. A conservative margin such as 10% may be useful for a particular treatment, but it is not an Apple-defined safe area. Set margins from the current template, intended crop and brief; annotate their source. A planned phone bleed is a documented layout choice, not permission for the headline or key UI to clip.

## Review the actual deliverable

Check the icon/name first, then the headline, then the product evidence. Inspect both the least and most spacious locales, plus every layout that differs. Confirm meaningful text contrast and keep editorial text clear of the device. Show exact-size previews in the review page instead of scaling every thumbnail to fill its container.

Deliver the requested locales and dimensions with per-image hashes and clear UI-language notes. Do not add price, offer, marketplace badge or web-address copy merely because it is common in social advertisements; use the current creative-asset guidance linked in the entrypoint.
